WPAForge is a Wi-Fi network auditing tool for Android. It verifies the WPA/WPA2 passphrase of a network you own or are authorised to test. Candidates are generated on the device from the target SSID, vendor and other hints, and every candidate is checked against a real association — a completed 4-way handshake is the only success criterion.

Everything runs locally: nothing is uploaded, and matched passphrases are stored on the device in encrypted form.

Features

• Real verification instead of guessing — every candidate triggers an actual association and is reported as success, wrong password, or inconclusive
• Candidates generated for the target from SSID, vendor, house number, names, birthdays and phone numbers
• Use the built-in dictionary, or import / download your own
• Dictionary page shows how each wordlist performs on your own targets
• WPS PIN attacks including split-halving, with resumable progress
• LAN reconnaissance: live hosts and common ports grouped by vendor, with an exportable report
• Offline verification of .cap / .hc22000 captures (EAPOL and PMKID paths)
• Spectrum radar and channel occupancy with recommendations
• Your existing network configuration is left alone: the original profile is recorded before anything is touched and written back with a read-back confirmation; unresolved cases stay in the Recovery section for retry

Requirements

• Android 8.0 or newer
• Root access. The verification path relies on a root shell plus `cmd wifi`. (Shizuku and no-root execution modes were removed in 1.9.24.)

Use only on networks you own or have explicit authorisation to test.
